# Daykit: seven-day launch playbook

## The target and the truth

100,000 visits in seven days is a breakout target, not a forecast. At a 4% click-through rate it requires roughly 2.5 million qualified impressions. The most credible route is one large distribution event (a top launch, creator mention, or community post) reinforced by many smaller channels. Product work and SEO improve conversion and compounding; they cannot guarantee reach in one week.

Primary message: **One bookmark. 29 private tools. No uploads, sign-up, ads, or watermarks.**

Primary URL: <https://solmyst.github.io/Daykit/>

## Launch scorecard

Use platform-native numbers so Daykit can retain its no-analytics promise.

| Stage | Seven-day target | How to measure |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified impressions | 2,500,000 | Post and creator dashboards |
| Link clicks | 100,000 | Platform link-click reports |
| GitHub interest | 1,000 stars | GitHub repository insights |
| Earned placements | 25 | Manual outreach tracker |
| User feedback | 50 actionable reports | GitHub Issues |

Visits cannot be verified precisely without analytics. Do not install tracking silently. If exact traffic measurement becomes essential, add a disclosed, cookieless analytics option and update the privacy copy first.

## Audience wedges

1. **Students:** PDF merge/split, word counter, notes, date and average calculators.
2. **Freelancers and small teams:** image compression/conversion, QR codes, PDFs, percentage calculations.
3. **Developers and makers:** JSON, CSV/JSON, Base64, URL encoding, UUIDs, hashes, timestamps.
4. **Privacy-conscious users:** local processing, no account, no upload queue, no watermark.

## Seven-day execution

### Day 0 — prepare

- Test the live homepage and top ten tools on desktop and mobile.
- Capture one 45-second demo and three short tool clips: merge PDF, compress image, format JSON.
- Create Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, and directory profiles.
- Build a list of 100 creators/newsletters covering productivity, student tools, privacy, indie software, and developer utilities.
- Ask 15 trusted testers for honest launch-day feedback. Do not coordinate fake votes.

### Day 1 — launch spike

- Publish on Product Hunt and Show HN.
- Post one transparent founder story on relevant Reddit communities only after checking each community’s current self-promotion rules.
- Publish the demo on X and LinkedIn; pin it.
- Send personal messages to the first 30 relevant creators. Lead with their audience’s use case, not a generic request.
- Reply to every substantive comment within 15 minutes during the first six hours.

### Day 2 — use-case distribution

- Publish three focused posts: “private PDF tools,” “student utility kit,” and “developer text toolbox.”
- Submit to AlternativeTo, Uneed, Microlaunch, Fazier, TinyLaunch, and other reputable utility directories.
- Contact university communities, coworking newsletters, bootcamps, and privacy communities with a tailored description.

### Day 3 — proof and iteration

- Turn real feedback into fixes and publish a short changelog.
- Share the most useful user quote only with permission.
- Pitch 30 more creators using the best-performing use case from launch day.

### Day 4 — tool-led SEO

- Link directly to individual tool pages rather than the homepage.
- Publish demonstrations answering exact tasks: “merge PDFs without uploading,” “compress an image privately,” and “format JSON locally.”
- Request indexing through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after verifying ownership.

### Day 5 — community utility

- Offer Daykit as a free resource in question threads where it genuinely solves the stated problem. Disclose affiliation.
- Publish an open roadmap and invite tool requests through GitHub Issues.
- Reach out to browser-extension, privacy, student, and productivity newsletters.

### Day 6 — second launch angle

- Launch a focused story around privacy: “29 tools that run on your device.”
- Share a transparent build breakdown on Indie Hackers and maker communities.
- Re-contact creators who opened or replied; do not spam non-responders.

### Day 7 — consolidate

- Publish the week’s outcomes, failures, fixes, and next three tools.
- Double down on the two channels with the highest link-click rate.
- Convert recurring questions into page copy and FAQ updates.

## Paid amplification guardrails

- **$0:** prioritize communities, launch platforms, directories, and personalized creator outreach.
- **$1,000:** sponsor 2–4 small, tightly matched newsletters or creators; avoid broad display ads.
- **$5,000:** test several creator placements with unique campaign links, then shift spend to the best click quality after 24 hours.
- Never buy votes, reviews, followers, backlinks, or undisclosed endorsements.

## Conversion checklist

- The post links to the exact tool shown in the demo.
- The first sentence names the task and privacy benefit.
- The shared image is legible on mobile.
- Every response is human, specific, and disclosed as coming from the maker.
- Feedback is turned into visible improvements quickly.
- No claim says Daykit can do OCR, Office layout conversion, PDF compression, or video transcoding until those features exist.

## After the launch week

Search growth will take longer than seven days. Expand around the highest-use tasks with original guides, accurate examples, internal links, and performance data. Prioritize requested tools that can remain reliable and browser-local; do not inflate the catalog with thin or duplicate utilities.
